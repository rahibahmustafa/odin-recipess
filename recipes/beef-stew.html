Beef stew

Beef stew is a very tasty stew made of meat pieces that can accompanied by any type of food.

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Red onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Green pepper and carrots
  • Garlic
  • Black pepper
  • Royco
  • Salt
  • cooking oil
  • Water

The steps

  1. wash and chop the beef into preferable sizes
  2. chop all your ingredients
  3. put your pan on heat and add some cooking oil
  4. put the meat in the pan and let it fry for a few minutes before adding the onions
  5. when it turns a bit brown, add the onions and garlic
  6. go on and add the carrots,green pepper and tomatoes
  7. once all the ingridients are ready, add in the salt,black pepper and royco
  8. conclude by adding water and let it boil for like 15 minutes and then your stew is ready!
