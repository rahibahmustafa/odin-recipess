Chapati

This is a dish made from wheatflour commonly enjoyed in East African countriea

Ingredients

  • wheatflour
  • warm water
  • salt
  • cooking oil

Steps

  1. mix the flour and salt
  2. add water gradually and knead
  3. add oil and knead till its smooth
  4. let the dough rest
  5. divide and roll into circles
  6. cook on a heated pan until golden brown on both sides
